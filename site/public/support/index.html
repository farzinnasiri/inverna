I bought Inverna but it’s asking me to buy again

On the purchase screen, tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. If Family Sharing is on, a family member’s purchase works too.

I don’t get bedtime reminders

Check that Bedtime reminder is on in Inverna’s Settings, and that notifications for Inverna are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications. Focus modes like Sleep can hold them back too.

The sound stops, or keeps playing

The sleep timer on Sounds decides when the sound fades out. End of my sleep time follows your usual sleep time; All night never stops by itself. A call or Siri pauses the sound, and it comes back afterwards unless the timer has run out.

Starting over

Settings → Erase all data clears everything on your phone and takes you back to the start. Your purchase isn’t affected.
