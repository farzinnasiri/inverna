Inverna

Privacy

Inverna is made to be quiet, and that includes your data. Almost everything stays on your phone.

Last updated 27 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no ads, and nothing sold or shared for advertising.
  • Your name, bedtime, check-in answers, sounds and games stay on your phone.
  • Anonymous usage and crash reports help me fix and improve the app. You can turn them off.
  • Erase all data in Settings removes everything Inverna keeps.

What stays on your phone

Everything you tell Inverna or make in it is stored only on your device: your first name if you share it, your usual sleep time, your answers in onboarding and the evening check-in, the sounds you save, your settings, game progress and best scores, and the times you finish a wind-down, which Inverna uses to learn your usual sleep time.

None of this is sent to me or anyone else. Like other app data, it can be included in your iPhone’s own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under your account.

Usage and crash reports

To see which parts of Inverna people use and to fix problems, the app sends anonymous reports: things like which screens are opened, how far onboarding goes, which features are used, roughly what time of day, and the details of any crash or error.

They carry a random ID made on your phone, not your name, email or Apple ID, and never your bedtime, check-in answers or anything you typed. Your rough country is worked out from your connection when a report arrives, and the IP address itself isn’t kept. Reports are stored with PostHog in the EU.

They’re on unless you untick the box at the end of onboarding, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings.

Reminders

Bedtime reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. They’re off until you turn them on, and they never go through a server. A reminder can include your first name, which you can leave out.

Buying Inverna

Inverna is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; I never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Apple that the purchase is yours.

Erasing your data

Settings → Erase all data removes everything Inverna keeps on your phone, including any reminders it scheduled. Deleting the app does the same. To have your usage reports removed too, write to me with the ID shown under Usage reports in Settings.

Not medical advice

Inverna is made to help you relax and wind down before bed. It isn’t a medical device, and it doesn’t diagnose, treat or promise to fix any sleep problem. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, please talk to a doctor.

Children

Inverna isn’t made for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect anything about them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date. Questions about your privacy are welcome: see Help and support for how to reach me.
