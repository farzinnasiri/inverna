# Inverna — 2027 Vision

> **North Star:** Earn **Apple Editors' Choice** in 2027 by building the calmest, most crafted evening wind-down instrument on iPhone and iPad — an app engineered to help you *put your phone down*, never to hook you.

## What Inverna stands for (non-negotiable)

- **Built to end, not hook you.** No feeds, no streaks, no leaderboards, no countdown panic, no ads. Everything softens and slows down as bedtime gets closer.
- **Craft over content bloat.** Watchmaker Ink & Brass OLED restraint, native iOS 26 Liquid Glass, real-time on-device synthesis, and tactile haptics that only answer the user's own hands.
- **Honest science, zero hype.** Grounded in visuospatial working-memory offloading, 1/f noise, slow acoustic descent, and low-blue OLED optics — always explained plainly, never pretending to be medicine.
- **Radical respect for privacy & wallet.** 100% offline, zero accounts, Apple **`Data Not Collected`**, and a fair one-time purchase (no `$70/yr` subscription trap).

## Release Arc (2026 → 2027)

### v1.0 — Trust, Ratings & Word-of-Mouth (Now)
- **Goal:** Land the first real user base, honest feedback, and a strong **4.8★+** rating foundation.
- **Model:** Generous free taste (4 games, 4 sounds, 1 custom sound in Composer, Adaptive night light, Wind-down) → single one-time Full Unlock (**`$6.99` US** + 4-tier PPP, Family Sharing on).
- **Shipped craft:** All 13 quiet classics, 12 procedural presets + 7-layer Composer, harmonic game-to-sound key lock, adaptive warm OLED night light, native StoreKit 2, and gentle `SKStoreReviewController` prompt.
- **Distribution:** Lock positioning (`Inverna: Sleep Games & Sounds`), nocturnal icon refresh, crisp App Store screenshots + hero banner, and authentic founder launch posts (`r/ADHD`, `r/nosurf`, `r/iosgaming`, X).

### v1.1 — Deep Apple-Ecosystem Delight
- **Interactive Lock Screen & Home Screen widgets** (one-tap sleep sound & wind-down start, matching the craft of *Dark Noise*).
- **Dynamic Island / Lock Screen Live Activity** for an active Wind-down (subtle dimming progress + quiet fade-out cue).
- **App Store Creative Assets:** Animated muted hero header loop + localized App Store listings for top non-US markets.
- **Accessibility polish:** Full VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion audit across every sheet, slider, and game.

### v1.2 — The Flagship Signature Experience (14th Game)
- **A truly original, unmistakable Inverna flagship game:** An infinite, Japanese-inspired low-arousal tactile flow experience (bridging *Pond*'s ink-wash craft, *Scroll to Sleep*'s effortless thumb-drift, and *Energy: Anti-Stress Loops*'s luminous geometry) that physically slows down with `calm` toward bedtime.
- Once ratings pass 50–100+ strong reviews, revisit US unlock pricing (`$6.99 → $9.99` sequential check via App Store Connect).

### v2.0 — 2027 Apple Editors' Choice Nomination
- Submit an **App Store Connect Featuring Nomination** 4–6 weeks ahead of the milestone release, highlighting:
  1. Unique hybrid category (*part quiet interactive ritual, part real-time acoustic synthesizer*).
  2. Native iOS 26/27 craft (Liquid Glass, Icon Composer, widgets, Live Activity, iPad layout, full accessibility).
  3. Authentic indie founder story + zero-tracking, zero-subscription integrity.
