# Lull app icon studies

[Open the gallery](index.html) · [View the contact sheet](contact-sheet.png)

All nine use the current Watchmaker Ink & Brass tokens on opaque OLED black. Each folder contains an editable SVG and the same mark exported at 1024, 60 and 40 pixels. The small-size notes describe standalone icon previews and anticipated compact use. Dynamic Island placement has not been tested on device.

## 01 · Dimmed Stone

One pewter tile catches a sliver of brass along its upper corner.

The tile could have come straight from a puzzle board. The curved edge gives Lull’s night light a physical surface to fall across.

**Compact use.** The tile remains solid at 40 px. The long taper becomes a short brass corner.

## 02 · Moon in the Grid

Three stones hold their places while a crescent fills the fourth square.

The missing tile gives the familiar bedtime moon a role in a game.

**Compact use.** The four positions remain distinct at 40 px, with open black space inside the moon.

## 03 · Lull Line

A brass wave makes one last dip and settles into a level line.

Its diminishing motion follows both a sleep sound fading and a slow game coming to rest.

**Compact use.** The crest and dip hold up at 40 px. The horizontal tail is short but visible.

## 04 · Last Wick

A low flame sits at the bottom of a tall, empty lantern.

The space above the wick suggests the last few minutes of the night light.

**Compact use.** The frame and flame survive at 40 px. The flame reads as a warm point at smaller sizes.

## 05 · Soft Landing

A single brass game piece hangs above a matching recess.

The unfinished move recalls the slow falling-block game that inspired Lull. The eye can finish the move without a motion trail.

**Compact use.** The open gap stays visible at 40 px. The block and socket also read in silhouette.

## 06 · Koi Turn

A brass koi curls back toward its pewter tail.

The koi pond gives Lull a living emblem. The space inside the turn suggests a fish circling slowly.

**Compact use.** The curved body and separate tail carry the shape at 40 px. The eye is secondary.

## 07 · Still Pendulum

A brass pendulum hangs vertical beside the last trace of its swing.

A watchmaker’s instrument gives the idea of slowing down a precise, tactile form.

**Compact use.** The bob and stem dominate at 40 px. The short grey arc becomes a secondary accent.

## 08 · Half Drawn

A round window goes dark in three horizontal bands.

The bands borrow the rhythm of completed puzzle rows and the diminishing light of the dimmer.

**Compact use.** The separated bands and domed top remain recognizable at 40 px.

## 09 · Quiet Circuit

A single rounded path spirals inward to a brass resting point.

It borrows the right-angle turns of Lull’s Thread puzzle and gives the eye a short route to follow.

**Compact use.** The black channels stay open at 40 px. The brass endpoint serves as the focal point.
