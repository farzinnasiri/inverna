## 1. Executive Summary

**Overall verdict: Hold App Store and Google Play submissions until P0/P1 blockers are fixed. Keep Inverna's core architecture — do not rewrite it.**

Inverna's foundational architecture (`src/core` purity, deterministic engines, Watchmaker tokens, Skia backdrop simulation, native Web Audio synthesis, shared `ambientClock`) is **genuinely strong**. Most bugs do not come from bad architecture or large files; they cluster around **six specific ownership seams**:

1. **Store & Release Blockers:** No in-app Privacy Policy (Apple & Google P0), unverified Google Play FGS/Health declarations (P0), overly strong physiological/sleep claims in Onboarding/Science (`beta → alpha/theta`, cortisol/dopamine, melatonin) risking Apple §§1.4.1/2.3, `Erase all data` leaving custom mix names resumable from the lock screen, and a manually-dispatched signing workflow (`.github/workflows/eas-build.yml`) that executes `eval` on dispatch inputs and can silently fall back to Android debug signing.
2. **Audio Sleep Deadline, Interruption & Debug-Build Noise:** The sleep fade (CoreAudio clock) and playback stop (JS 1s interval) have split ownership — if iOS suspends JS while locked, Inverna can fade to silence yet keep synthesizing 80–143 audio nodes indefinitely. Editing a mix during the final 12s fade swells volume back to 1.0, and phone calls/Siri can extend a fixed timer or restart audio *after* wind-down finishes. Meanwhile, the owner's dev-build charging crackle has a concrete Debug-only suspect in `react-native-audio-api`: `AudioThreadGuard` runs `getrusage(RUSAGE_SELF)` 187.5×/s on the audio thread in Debug builds (excluded in Release).
3. **Notification / Reminder Concurrency & DST Bugs:** Unserialized async reminder planning can resurrect 6 notifications *after* the user disables reminders or erases all data; UTC-day flooring causes a DST spring-forward ID collision (`inverna-winddown-20541` on two consecutive nights in `Europe/Rome`); skipping onboarding still requests notification permission because reminders default to `true`.
4. **Game Lifecycle & Input Jank:** `GameShell` infers door/pause/ready/new-game state from fragile flags — opening **How to play** from the door discards the door and softlocks start-gated games; first-launch difficulty changes are ignored; `Settle` and `Hue` overwrite and save the board immediately on mode/difficulty tap; `Chime` drops Classic mode on checkpoint and stalls when resuming a cleared Endless wall; `Cascade` blocks taps for ~1.6s until debris disappears and reconciles its entire SVG board on React state every frame; `Nine`'s in-game "New puzzle" strands the board; `Pond` resets unfinished pictures on minor layout aspect shifts (0.08) and omits the loop gesture from its door text.
5. **Web PWA Defects:** `public/sw.js` deletes *all* other caches on the origin and serves HTML on offline JS/WASM misses; `NativeTabs` renders a floating top tab bar on web that visually overlaps the `Sounds` screen's top utility pills (`Sleep timer` and `Composer / Hz`) on both phone and iPad viewports.
6. **Startup & Idle Thermal Leaks:** Native launch eagerly synthesizes all 9 stereo noise textures (~15.0 MiB, 30–41ms JS turns) via two uncoordinated warmers that can generate textures twice; the opaque `Goodnight` curtain fails to register as a cover or pause running games; Reduce Motion keeps a 30 Hz backdrop timer ticking; Onboarding/MindTangle/Science bypass `ambientClock`.

## 4. Sound System, DSP, Thermal/Charging Noise & Patch Audit

### 4.1 Why Dev Builds Crackle When Hot/Charging — And Why Battery Can Drain Overnight

1. **Dev-Build Charging Noise (Concrete Debug-Only Suspect — Device Verification Required):**
   - `node_modules/react-native-audio-api/RNAudioAPI.podspec:132` compiles with `DEBUG=1` in Debug builds and excludes `AudioThreadGuard.cpp` from Release builds.
   - In Debug, `BaseAudioContext.cpp:131` constructs `AudioThreadGuard` on **every 128-frame audio render quantum**, and `AudioThreadGuard.cpp:46` calls `getrusage(RUSAGE_SELF)` on Apple platforms. At Inverna's 24 kHz context rate, that is **187.5 `getrusage` syscalls per second on the real-time audio render thread** alongside unoptimized C++ DSP and Metro/Hermes contention. Under thermal throttling + charging (when iOS lowers efficiency-core headroom), missed CoreAudio render deadlines manifest as crackle/noise — explaining why the owner observes it on **Inverna Dev** while charging, not on **Inverna Beta (Release)**.
   - Inverna already requests `sampleRate: 24000` and `latencyHint: 'playback'` (~4,096 frames / ~85 ms preference on iOS). Keep those settings during diagnosis, but fix the inaccurate comment at [sound.native.ts:139](file:///Users/farzin/MyProjects/inverna/src/platform/sound.native.ts#L139) (the warmth filter is a high-shelf, and pads/binaural/cues bypass the texture warmth bus).
2. **Confirmed Overnight Battery Culprit (Reproduced in Harness):**
   - When a sleep timer expires while the phone is locked, the CoreAudio `sleepFadeGain` ramp reaches `0`, **but the audio graph keeps synthesizing** until React's 1s `SessionCurtain` timer runs `stop()` + teardown timers + `ctx.suspend()`. With JS suspended on a locked phone, Theta Rain stayed `isRunning === true` with 4 looping sources and **81 active nodes** (up to **143 nodes** in full custom + Nocturne mixes) rendering silence continuously.

### 7.3 Seven Laws of Software Engineering Applied
1. **Tesler's Law:** Overlay/glass complexity in `Layer` and `GlassSurface` is irreducible — keep it centralized rather than pushing it into callers.
2. **Law of Leaky Abstractions:** `readJSON<T>` lies about runtime validation, forcing screens to defensively re-validate (and crashing `index.tsx` if `playLayout.order` is missing). Decode once from `unknown`.
3. **DRY (Intent, Not Appearance):** The duplicated 700ms slider sync hack in `PillSlider` and `Slider` duplicates a broken assumption — extract one correct reconciliation helper. Conversely, keep game-specific gesture geometry local.
4. **KISS:** Fixing the reminder race needs a single serialized writer + monotonic token, not a job queue framework.
5. **Principle of Least Astonishment:** Swiping a paused sound must select what Play starts; dragging a sheet closed must run the same close handler as tapping the backdrop.
6. **Goodhart's Law:** Never impose a file-length cap (which would vandalize `sim.ts`); measure change-amplification (how many files break for one feature — e.g., Sound studio & Game #14).
7. **The Map Is Not the Territory:** Outdated `ARCHITECTURE.md` and native-only `tsc` mislead agents about the actual Expo RN + Web PWA territory.
