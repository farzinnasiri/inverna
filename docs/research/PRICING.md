# Inverna US launch pricing

**Launch at $14.99 once, with no introductory discount.** The two comparisons I would give most weight are A Little to the Left at $9.99 for a full puzzle game and myNoise at $19.99 for full access to a customizable sound library. Inverna offers both play and sound in one bedtime routine. The reason to stay below $19.99 at launch is that buyers must decide before they have tried either. This is a pricing judgment. ## What the evidence supports

- **Observed hard-paywall conversion.** RevenueCat’s 2026 subscription-app sample had median download-to-paid conversion by day 35 of **10.7%** for hard-paywall apps and **2.1%** for freemium. Different apps chose each model, and subscription trials can precede payment. This comparison cannot isolate the effect of adding a paywall or forecast Inverna’s immediate one-time purchase rate. [RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/)
- **Observed one-time price cut.** Tiger Style reported $12,946 over 15 days at $4.99 for Waking Mars and $25,733 over the next 15 days at $2.99. Apple featuring and publicity overlapped the sale, the spike faded, and the developer ended the discount. Its second game, Spider, saw a much smaller benefit. This is historical paid-download revenue data without an install denominator. I found no controlled conversion curve for a comparable post-onboarding lifetime unlock or evidence for a specific launch-discount duration. [Developer’s sales figures and commentary](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/tiger-style-outlines-how-ios-price-drops-affect-overall-sales-data)
- **Decision arithmetic.** At equal traffic and commission, gross revenue per download is price × purchase conversion. To match $14.99, a $4.99 unlock needs **3.00×** as many buyers and $9.99 needs **1.50×**. A $19.99 unlock needs **75.0%** as many buyers and $29.99 needs **50.0%**. These are break-even thresholds calculated from the candidate prices, not measured demand.

## Three launch options

| US price | Judgment | What would justify it |
|---|---|---|
| $9.99 | Conservative. Easier first purchase before someone has tried a game or heard a mix. | Choose if acquiring more paying users is the priority, or a comparable cohort buys about 50% more often than at $14.99. |
| **$14.99** | **Recommended.** Between a full puzzle unlock and a dedicated sound composer. A buyer can justify the purchase through repeated use of both. | Establish the baseline at the price intended for ordinary sales. |
| $19.99 | Premium. Captures more from people already seeking an offline evening routine, but asks the buyer to trust that routine sight unseen. | Prefer it if purchase conversion holds above 75% of the $14.99 level on comparable traffic. |

**Introductory price: none (zero days).** A $9.99 launch sale would need about half again as many buyers just to match $14.99 revenue. Launch publicity also makes a discounted first week a poor baseline. If revisiting the decision, compare US unlock sales and proceeds per first-time US download over periods with similar acquisition sources, using aggregate App Store Connect reporting. [Developer sale case](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/tiger-style-outlines-how-ios-price-drops-affect-overall-sales-data) · [Apple App Analytics](https://developer.apple.com/app-store-connect/analytics/)

## Apple mechanics

- Apple offers up to **800 price points by default** and **100 additional high points on request**. The US range spans **$0.29 to $10,000**. Choose the US base price and Apple generates comparable storefront prices accounting for exchange rates and taxes. Apple keeps the base price fixed and adjusts automatically managed regions as conditions change. Developers may override individual storefronts. [Apple IAP pricing](https://developer.apple.com/in-app-purchase/) · [Set a price for an IAP](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-in-app-purchases/set-a-price-for-an-in-app-purchase/)
- For a standard US Apple-billed non-consumable, commission is **30%**, or **15%** once approved for the Small Business Program. Eligibility generally requires prior-calendar-year proceeds of **$1 million or less** across associated accounts, or being a new developer. If proceeds cross the threshold during the year, the standard rate applies to subsequent sales. On $14.99 that leaves about **$10.49** or **$12.74** respectively, before tax adjustments and refunds. [Paid Applications Agreement, Schedule 2 §3.4](https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/terms/schedules/Schedule-2-and-3-English.pdf) · [Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/)
- The price is reversible for future buyers. Change the existing IAP’s price schedule in App Store Connect. A temporary change has start and end dates and reverts automatically. A permanent base-price change can generate new regional prices. Existing owners keep the non-consumable and can restore it. [Schedule IAP price changes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-in-app-purchases/schedule-price-changes-for-in-app-purchases/) · [Apple purchase types](https://developer.apple.com/in-app-purchase/)
